Privacy Policy — OBS VOD Uploader

Last updated: October 13, 2026

OBS VOD Uploader is a personal tool used only by its author to upload their own stream recordings to their own YouTube channel.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos to the signed-in YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to that channel, used only to confirm which channel is signed in and to check whether an uploaded video has finished processing.

What it stores

The sign-in token is stored only on the author's own computer. No data is collected, sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API, sold, or shared with anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
